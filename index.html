How I lead

Clarity, ownership, and teams that act.

I chose to move from large-scale P&L and people management back toward hands-on product engineering. I want to understand the functional, technical and operational workflow well enough to ask the questions that move delivery.

Hire for intent to act

I look for a strong intention to take action and proactive communication, validated through real project deep-dives and references.

Domain-shaped teams

Teams own vertical domain slices using domain-driven design, so ownership maps to the business, not the tech layer.

Clarity, then coaching

Measurable expectations, coaching to execute, impediments removed, and deliberate learning from past outcomes.

Honest, specific feedback

Concrete examples of what to do better, so people always know where they stand and how to grow.

Data-driven prioritization

A Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF) framework gives leadership a transparent, consistent basis for investment decisions across business verticals.

Aligned execution

I align executives and cross-functional teams on roadmaps, dependencies and competing priorities so everyone executes against the same strategic objectives.
